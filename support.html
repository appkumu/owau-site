Common questions

When does Owau launch?

Owau is coming soon to iPhone. Join the waitlist and we'll email you on launch day.

Is Owau available on Android?

Not yet. We're launching on iOS first, with Android planned after.

How do affirmations in my own voice work?

You record a few words and phrases once. Owau uses them to create affirmations you can play back anytime. You can re-record or delete your recordings whenever you like.

What if I don't want to use my own voice?

You can choose one of Owau's guide voices instead.

What are portal dates?

Dates like 10/10, 11/11 and 12/12 that many manifestors use for setting intentions. Owau marks them on your calendar and can remind you the day before.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to [Settings → Account → Delete account]. This deletes your account, recordings and content. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Owau.
