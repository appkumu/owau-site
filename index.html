How it works

A practice that sounds like you.

Three steps to set up, then a few minutes a day.

1

Set your intention

Tell Owau what you're calling in first, like love, money or time freedom, and how you like to manifest: by seeing, hearing or doing.

2

Record your voice

Say a few words and phrases once. Owau builds affirmations you can play back in your own voice, or pick a guide voice instead.

3

Practice daily

Start each day with a short ritual, keep your streak going, and log every win along the way.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

When does Owau launch?

Owau is coming soon to iPhone. Join the waitlist and we'll email you on launch day.

Is Owau on Android?

Not yet. We're launching on iPhone first, with Android planned after.

How do affirmations in my own voice work?

You record a few words and phrases once, and Owau uses them to create affirmations you can play anytime. You can re-record or delete your recordings whenever you like.

What if I don't want to use my own voice?

Choose one of Owau's guide voices instead. You can switch anytime.

Is my voice private?

Your recordings are used only to create your affirmations, and you can delete them at any time. See our Privacy Policy for details.
